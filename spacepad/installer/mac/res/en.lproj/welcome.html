Welcome to Spacepad

Spacepad is a pad and crowd player for the stage. Choose your sounds folder: Spacepad analyzes it, keeps its name, finds the key of every sound and saves it to its Pad Library or Crowd Library.

This installer puts the app in Applications, along with the Audio Unit plugin (Logic Pro, GarageBand) and the VST3 plugin (Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One…).

Your sounds are never moved or deleted.
