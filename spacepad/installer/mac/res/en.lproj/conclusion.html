Spacepad is installed

1. Open Spacepad from the Applications folder.

2. Click “Choose a folder…” and select your sounds folder. Spacepad analyzes it; then click Save.

3. In Logic Pro, Ableton or GarageBand, open the JB Praise: Spacepad instrument plugin: your libraries are already there.

If macOS refuses to open the app the first time, right-click “Spacepad”, then choose Open.

To uninstall: open Uninstall Spacepad in the Applications folder (“Désinstaller Spacepad”).
